# Conversion Sales Rulebook — 営業マンとして書く

2026-05-09 user directive. This rulebook changes how agents should judge TOP,
`/money`, `/lots`, product, voice, reserve/contact, and every conversion page.

The site must not behave like a beautiful company brochure. It must behave like a
salesperson: stop the reader, name the situation in their head, show the risk of
moving without clarity, give proof, and offer the next lowest-friction action.

This document is strategy and persuasion guidance. It never overrides
`BRAND-TRUTH.md`: canonical facts, forbidden claims, copy compliance, photo rules,
and CTA policy still win.

## 0. Non-Negotiable Frame

The enemy is not a named competitor. The enemy is **opaque house-building**:

- building-price-only ads that hide total cost
- land, exterior work, ground improvement, bridge-loan cost, registration, loan fees,
  furniture, moving, and other costs appearing late
- showroom / ad / intermediary costs being silently folded into the buyer's long loan
- the buyer moving forward before seeing land-included total cost and monthly payment

Agents must write against that enemy. Do not start with Yamato's virtues. Start with
the reader's unresolved buying situation.

Use a compliance-safe form:

- Good: "建物価格だけで判断する前に、土地代・外構・諸費用まで見てください。"
- Good: "後から増えやすい費用を、契約前に確認します。"
- Good: "展示場維持費・広告費・中間マージンが、価格に乗りやすい構造があります。"
- Avoid: "騙される", "搾取", named competitor attacks, unprovable blanket claims.

## 1. Ten Rules

1. **Name the buyer's unresolved situation before presenting Yamato.**
   Do not begin with brand beauty, model names, awards, or company self-introduction.
   Begin with the concrete condition: no land yet, total cost unclear, monthly payment
   unknown, or concern about later additions.

2. **Name the enemy.**
   The enemy is not another company by name. It is the opaque cost structure of home
   building: building-only prices, late add-ons, showroom cost, ad cost, intermediary
   margins, and unclear total budgets. Write the page so the reader feels why clarity
   matters now.

3. **Translate every number into the buyer's meaning.**
   Never leave numbers as company trophies. Canonical numbers from `BRAND-TRUTH.md`
   must answer "so what for me?" Example: not just "600棟以上"; show that Yamato has
   faced many families' land / budget / monthly-payment decisions.

4. **Use testimonial stories only when they have tension.**
   The useful pattern is Before -> conflict/comparison -> decision trigger -> After.
   Weak praise such as "理想通りで気に入っています" should not be used as primary proof
   unless it is supported by the pre-purchase anxiety and decision context.

5. **FAQ is a sales battleground, not an appendix.**
   The biggest fear must be visible and answered boldly near the core of the page.
   On money pages, the key fear is late cost increase / total cost opacity. Do not bury
   it inside a small accordion row.

6. **CTA must be a staircase.**
   Do not present LINE, documents, and visit booking as equal choices. The sequence is:
   ultra-low-friction diagnosis / simulator -> LINE proposal -> document request ->
   visit booking. Every conversion page should have one primary action and one clear
   secondary action.

7. **Create urgency through operations, not fake pressure.**
   If facts are available, show remaining lots, recent movement, monthly consultation
   slots, or current land candidates. If facts are not available, design the data slot
   and operational workflow instead of inventing urgency.

8. **Hero copy must create a reason to keep reading.**
   The hero is not a descriptor. It must answer: what is this, who is it for, and why
   should the reader continue now? Use one of these patterns:
   story, contrast, or anxiety-resolution.

9. **Photos should sell life, not only architecture.**
   Prefer approved real Yamato photos that imply daily life: table, kitchen, family
   movement, morning light, lived-in scale. Exterior beauty alone is secondary unless
   the section is specifically about land / building proof.

10. **Write as a salesperson, not a curator.**
    The evaluation question is: after reading alone for 30 minutes, would the reader
    want their own total budget answer? If not, the page is too passive.

## 2. Required Page Order For Sales Pages

For TOP and money-related pages, default to this order unless there is a strong reason:

1. Situation/fear in the reader's words
2. The opaque-cost enemy
3. Yamato's reversal: land-included total cost and monthly payment first
4. Mechanism: self-owned lots, integrated land/design/build/sales, no dedicated
   showroom burden, necessary-minimum advertising, standard specs, ground/bridge/fee
   handling
5. Proof: canonical facts translated into buyer benefit
6. Product/model introduction
7. Case or testimonial with Before -> conflict -> decision -> After
8. CTA staircase

Product explanations before the fear and mechanism are usually premature.

## 3. Hero Copy Patterns

Use one of these patterns before designing.

### Story

Start with a family who nearly gave up or could not see total cost, then show why the
answer changed.

### Contrast

Compare building-only price thinking with land-included total-cost thinking.

### Anxiety Resolution

State the concrete risk, then promise the first action that reduces it. Use situation
language, not emotion-labeling. For example, prefer:

"土地も総額もまだ見えていないご家族へ。"

over:

"不安ですよね。"

## 4. CTA Staircase

The preferred hierarchy for sales pages:

1. Primary: "無料で土地込み総額を診断する" or equivalent self-answer action
2. Secondary: "モデルハウスで標準仕様を見る"
3. Tertiary: LINE for a concrete answer, preferably tied to area / budget / lots
4. Quaternary: document request

When a page uses LINE as the primary channel, the CTA must still promise a concrete
answer, not vague "相談".

## 5. Before Shipping Checklist

- Does the first screen name the reader's situation before introducing Yamato?
- Is the enemy clear: opaque total cost, not a named competitor?
- Are numbers translated into buyer meaning?
- Is the strongest fear visible outside an accordion?
- Is there one primary CTA and one secondary CTA?
- Does the primary CTA offer an answer, not just contact?
- Are testimonials story-shaped or omitted?
- Is urgency factual or designed as a real operational data slot?
- Are claims compatible with `BRAND-TRUTH.md` forbidden assertions?
- Would a reader feel "I should get my own total cost answer"?
